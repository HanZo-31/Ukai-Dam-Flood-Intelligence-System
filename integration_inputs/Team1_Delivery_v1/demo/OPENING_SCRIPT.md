# 90-second opening script (reference exploration plus stored What-if)

1. (0:00) "Dam operators ask: if the release changes, how does the flood change, how soon does it arrive, and which areas should we look at first?"
2. (0:10) Explore, H02, depth view. Point at the "simulated, uncalibrated" label. "HEC-RAS gives us a reference library of four stored scenarios."
3. (0:25) Timeline: drag to 24 h, 60 h, the end. "This is the area reached by elapsed model time, in hourly steps. Nothing here uses a real clock."
4. (0:40) Compare: left H02 = baseline, right H03 = candidate. "Candidate minus baseline: this much newly flooded area, by depth above 10 cm." Click a flagged zone.
5. (0:55) "Zones are 5 km coordinate squares. 'Priority for review' orders them: new flooded area first, then earlier arrival. It is not an evacuation order."
6. (1:05) What-if: choose 20,000 m³/s, press Look up. "This selects an existing HEC-RAS result. Nothing is computed, no ML." Point at engine, requested vs actual peak, lookup-time note.
7. (1:20) Evidence: "Everything is simulated, uncalibrated and not validated; the methods and limits are written here."
Do not say: "real-time", "prediction", "validated", or quote the lookup time as model speed.
